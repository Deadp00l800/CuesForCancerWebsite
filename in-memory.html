The Ghost Light

In theater tradition, a stage is never left fully dark. A single lamp — the ghost light — stays lit through the night: a light for those who are no longer there to walk the stage, and a light to guide those who are yet to come.

Here, each light belongs to someone our community has loved. Share a name and their story, and a light is placed for them — one that stays lit, always.

"The stage remembers everyone who ever stood on it."
