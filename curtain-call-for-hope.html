Inaugural Benefit Concert & Silent Auction

A Curtain Call for Hope

A hybrid benefit concert — one night, one purpose, every heart.

The Event

A Night That Moves People

An unforgettable evening of live performance, community, and giving at one of Washington, D.C.'s most iconic venues.

Saturday, November 14, 2026

6:00 PM – 9:30 PM

The Pavilion — Second Floor

Ronald Reagan Building & International Trade Center, Washington, D.C.

  • Cocktail Hour6:00 – 7:00 PM
  • Silent Auction6:00 – 8:15 PM
  • Benefit Concert7:30 – 9:30 PM

"For one night, the weight of a diagnosis becomes shared — and that changes everything."

Featured Artist

A Performance & Conversation with Jai'Len Josey

A Curtain Call for Hope proudly features Jai'Len Josey, an acclaimed professional performing artist. Joining the program from her national tour, Jai'Len brings a featured performance and an intimate interview on how the arts inspire hope and transform lives.
